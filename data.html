What we changed

  • Kept only the fields the tool uses: screen, body and stand sizes, curve, panel, ergonomics, ports, power and weight.
  • Converted sizes to whole millimetres and weights to kilograms.
  • Where Icecat lists no screen area, worked it out from the diagonal and resolution; those values are marked "estimated".
  • Merged regional variants of the same model into one entry.
  • A few monitors were entered by hand and are not from Icecat.
