The monitor's bottom edge sits about 9 cm above the desk. Expect ±2–3 cm, because the shelf front is closer to the camera than the screen.
  • Height: every monitor is drawn with its bottom edge at your current 9 cm, so you can compare tops. Real stands differ.
  • Sizes are manufacturer body dimensions without stand. Your 27" is taken as the PA279CRV (the PA279CV is 2.5 mm wider). ProArt 32" body height and the Dell 40"/LG 49"/Dell 52" panel sizes are worked out from diagonal and ratio. Curved screens are drawn at their spec width.
  • The door frame trim is shallow, so a monitor standing in front of it can overlap it. The shelf is a hard stop.
  • Chair view assumes the stand's base starts at the distance you set from the desk's back edge, the screen face sits 60% of the stand's depth forward of that, eyes 45 cm above the desk top and a 30 cm deep shelf unit. Viewing-angle bands are a rule of thumb: up to about 45° is easy to take in, past 60° you turn your head.
